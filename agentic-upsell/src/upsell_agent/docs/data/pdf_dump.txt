 Developer Implementation Specification | Version 1 | September 2026
Core operating principle
Every new sales lead remains actively worked until contact produces a defined next action, an
appointment, an opt-out/suppression event, a Sales Visit, or the opportunity reaches Day 91.
Bucket-specific qualification logic may differ, but this state machine applies regardless of lead
bucket.
Compliance: Every automated SMS/email/AI action and human call task remains subordinate to AutoPulse
communication-permission, opt-out, dealer schedule, time-window, frequency, and suppression guardrails.

2. Global Transition Rules
 One controlling workflow: a customer/opportunity cannot simultaneously run incompatible Short-Term, Specific Follow-Up,
Appointment, and No-Show flows.
 Status change cancels stale work: cancel/pause future messages and human tasks from the prior status before starting the
new workflow.
 Appointment wins: any appointment creation immediately sets Appointment Set.
 Sales Visit wins: any qualifying SALES check-in immediately sets Sales Visit and stops lead/appointment/no-show
automation.
 Specific timing wins: 'call me Friday/next month/in a year' creates a dated future action rather than continuing aggressive
Short-Term cadence.
 Opt-out wins: immediately suppress the affected communication(s) under the compliance policy.
 Master opportunity age never resets: re-entry may restart a cadence instance, but original opportunity_created_at continues
toward Day 91.
 Pre-send recheck: immediately before any queued message/task executes, re-read current status, reply state, appointment
state, Sales Visit state, suppression state, and compliance result.
3. Short-Term Follow-Up
Triggers: New Lead, No Contact Made, Contact Made - No Next Action, and completion of the defined Appointment No-Show
sequence.
Days 1-7: Day 1 has Touch 1 plus the mandatory three-hour Touch 2. Days 2-7 have one additional SMS + email touch per
day. Human agents receive two call tasks per scheduled workday for seven days.

Global Human Call Task Escalation Rule
Required addition to all AutoPulse follow-up workflows in this specification
NON-NEGOTIABLE GLOBAL RULE
Every time AutoPulse schedules an automated text/email follow-up touch, the system must
also schedule a human call-task checkpoint for 1 hour after that AI touch.
If meaningful customer contact occurs before the 1-hour checkpoint, cancel the call task.
If no meaningful contact occurs, create/activate the human call task.
Required Developer Logic
1. AI SMS/email touch is successfully sent.
2. Start a 60-minute connection timer tied to that specific outreach event.
3. During the 60 minutes, watch for meaningful customer contact by AI or human.
4. If meaningful contact occurs, cancel the pending call task.
5. If no meaningful contact occurs at 60 minutes, create/activate a human call task.
6. Call task must identify the customer, vehicle/opportunity, last AI touch, and current status.
7. Human can Click-to-Call where available or mark the call completed manually.
8. After call completion, immediately prompt the human for the required AutoPulse outcome/status.
9. If the call produces an appointment, Appointment Set is applied automatically and its workflow starts.
10. If the call produces a specific future action, enter Contact Made - Specific Follow-Up.
11. If contact occurs but no clear next action exists, enter Contact Made - No Next Action.
12. If there is no contact, remain in the applicable no-contact/follow-up workflow.
13. If the customer opts out, apply the appropriate suppression immediately.
Where This Rule Applies
 Days 1-7 Short-Term Follow-Up AI text/email touches.
 Weekly Days 8-30 text/email outreach.
 Monthly Days 31-90 text/email outreach.
 Contact Made - Specific Follow-Up when the AI sends the scheduled text/email.
 Appointment and Appointment No-Show text/email touches when human follow-up is appropriate.
Important scheduling guardrail
The 1-hour human call task must also respect the human agent's scheduled work hours/days and
the applicable communication/compliance rules. If the 1-hour checkpoint falls outside an
allowed calling period or the agent's schedule, move the task to the next eligible call window.
Before activating the task, re-check current status so stale call tasks do not fire after a reply,
appointment, Sales Visit, opt-out, or other workflow-changing event.

Global Omnichannel Follow-Up Rule
Required controlling rule for every follow-up workflow in this specification
NON-NEGOTIABLE: ALL FOLLOW-UP = CALL + TEXT + EMAIL
Whenever a follow-up is due, AutoPulse must engage all available permitted channels:
AI text + AI email + human call task. Call, text, and email are not optional alternatives
unless a channel is unavailable, invalid, suppressed, or legally blocked.
Standard Follow-Up Sequence
1. At the scheduled follow-up time, AI sends the contextually appropriate SMS and email.
2. Start a 60-minute connection timer after the outbound AI touch.
3. If meaningful customer contact occurs during that hour, cancel the pending human call task.
4. If no meaningful connection occurs after 60 minutes, activate the human call task.
5. The call task supports Click-to-Call where available and requires completion plus outcome.
6. Appointment -> Appointment Set. Defined future action -> Contact Made - Specific Follow-Up.
7. Contact without clear next action -> Contact Made - No Next Action. No contact -> remain in applicable flow.
8. Opt-out/suppression immediately removes any prohibited channel from future touches.
Applies Everywhere Follow-Up Occurs
 Days 1-7 Short-Term Follow-Up
 Days 8-30 weekly follow-up
 Days 31-90 monthly follow-up
 Contact Made - Specific Follow-Up
 Appointment follow-up and confirmation touches
 Appointment No-Show follow-up
 Any later workflow that calls for customer follow-up unless that workflow explicitly stops outreach
Channel Availability & Compliance
'All channels' means all channels valid and permitted for that customer at that moment. If SMS is opted out, do not text.
If email is invalid/unsubscribed, do not email. If calling is blocked or outside the eligible window, do not call then.
Continue every remaining permitted channel.
Developer Override Rule
This omnichannel rule supersedes earlier wording in this PDF that describes follow-up as 'call/text/email,' 'text/email,' or
a choice among channels. For implementation, every follow-up means CALL + TEXT + EMAIL, subject only to current
status, connection state, valid channel data, suppression/opt-out, scheduling, and the AutoPulse compliance engine.

The AI must never decide whether a communication is legally permitted. Every outbound

3. Opt-Out / Revocation Handling
AutoPulse should suppress automated marketing immediately when a valid revocation is detected, even where an outside
legal deadline could permit additional processing time.
Explicit triggers: STOP, END, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE, QUIT and equivalent commands supported by the messaging
provider.
Natural-language triggers: examples include 'don't text me,' 'don't call me,' 'leave me alone,' 'remove my number,' 'take
me off your list,' and 'no more messages.'
Do not automatically equate a sales objection with revocation. 'I'm not interested' can be an objection rather than a
request to cease communications. If context makes revocation ambiguous, use REVIEW and stop automated marketing
while unresolved.
A permitted opt-out confirmation, where used, must be non-marketing and limited to confirming the revocation. Counsel
should approve the exact implementation.

identification/disclosure, opt-out, state-law and recordkeeping implementation.
