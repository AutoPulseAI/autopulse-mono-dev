1. WHEN THE AI WORKS AT ALL (per dealership)
================================================================================
The AI handles a dealership's customers only when ALL of these are true:

  1. AI Settings = "On" (live)                          [CRM: users.ai_mode]
       Off    -> the old CRM auto-replies (n8n) run, the AI does nothing
       Shadow -> the old auto-replies run; the AI drafts but sends nothing
  2. Auto-reply enabled                                  [users.setting.autoReplyEnabled]
     (off = the AI is off too, whatever the mode says)
  3. Subscription active                                 [users.package_expiry]
     (expired = the CRM refuses the dealership's incoming texts)
  4. A Twilio number registered for business texting (10DLC), whose incoming
     texts go to https://www.autopulse.ai/api/system/sms
     (watch for a Twilio Messaging Service overriding the number's webhook)
  5. Dealer Setup -> "SMS conversion phone" = that number
                                     [users.dealer_account_information.sms_conversion_phone]
     How an incoming text finds the dealership, and the number replies go from.
  6. Dealer Setup has opening hours and a time zone       [weekly_availability, time_zone]
     (missing: the AI uses Mon-Sat 9:00-18:00 for its own timing only and
     never tells customers any hours)

Nothing else is per dealership: the server settings (section 16) apply to all.
A new dealership works as soon as 1-6 are true.

2. HOW A MESSAGE FLOWS, END TO END
================================================================================
Customer texts the dealership:
  1. Twilio receives it -> POST https://www.autopulse.ai/api/system/sms
  2. CRM (aidmvcs-be-v2) finds the dealer by sms_conversion_phone; checks the
     subscription; queues the message (BullMQ / Redis "communicationQueue")
  3. CRM worker (aidmvcs-worker-v2) reads the dealer's AI mode:
       live   -> finds or creates the lead + customer, saves the message,
                 sends the event to the AI service (POST /v1/events/...)
       shadow -> old n8n reply goes out; AI gets the event marked shadow
       off    -> old n8n reply only
  4. AI API (agentic-upsell, 127.0.0.1:8100) accepts the event, queues a turn
     (Redis queue) - events that fail are stored and replayed when it's back
  5. AI worker (agentic-worker) runs the turn:
       load context  (dealer, customer 360, inventory, conversation, rules)
       extract       (what the customer said: slots, questions, intent) - OpenAI
       decide        (answer / ask / offer visit / book / handoff / stop / wait)
       compose       (the text + email) - OpenAI
       guard         (code checks: numbers, prices, links, claims, length)
       compliance    (opt-out, DND, state hours, caps) -> ALLOW / HOLD / BLOCK
       send          -> CRM POST /api/internal/ai/messages/send
  6. CRM sends through Twilio from the dealer's number (and email through its
     mail provider), and logs it in the lead's conversation
  7. Twilio delivery status -> CRM -> AI (/v1/.../status)

Scheduled work (follow-ups, call tasks, appointment reminders, sold workflows,
recalls, maintenance, birthdays, price checks) runs from the AI worker's
clock, every minute to every few hours, through the same steps 5-7.

Lead from a portal email / web form: the CRM creates the lead, then step 3 on.

3. WHERE THE DATA COMES FROM
================================================================================
A. Dealer profile - CRM users collection (Dealer Setup form), cached 60 s
   name / store_name                 how the AI introduces the dealership
   store_address, store_city,        address, "City, ST" in the first touch
     store_state, store_postal
   store_contact_number /            phone told to customers
     alternative_contact_number
   store_website                     website (only when asked)
   credit_finance_application_url    credit application link (only when asked)
   trade_in_appraisal_url            trade-in appraisal link (only when asked)
   weekly_availability, time_zone    opening hours, open/closed, after hours,
                                     call windows, staff default schedule
   sms_conversion_phone              number texts come in on / go out from
   ai_bot_name                       the AI's name ("Nicole") if set
   booking_capacity                  appointments per slot, slot length
   ai_daily_call_tasks               Days 1-7 call tasks on/off
   ai_mode, setting.autoReplyEnabled AI on / shadow / off
   ai_mms_enabled                    vehicle photos in texts on/off

B. Staff - CRM users with parent_id = the dealer
   name, role, work_schedule         who gets call tasks; days off

C. Customer and lead - CRM, mostly through Customer 360
   (GET /api/customers/{id}/360, which assembles):
   customers                         name, phones, emails, ZIP, consent
   leads                             source, comments, vehicle of interest,
                                     assigned salesperson, CRM status, DND
   emails                            the message history (texts + emails)
   bookings                          appointments
   deals                             sold vehicles, delivery date/mileage (DealerVault)
   repairorders                      service visits, mileage (DealerVault)
   serviceappointments               service bookings (DealerVault)
   tradeins                          trade-in vehicles on file

D. Inventory - the dealer's live stock: CRM GET /api/car
   year, make, model, trim, colour, miles, photos, page link,
   listed price (internetreduced) -> rough range told to the customer
   Searched per turn, cached briefly; prices snapshotted to detect price drops.

E. Outside services
   OpenAI (gpt-5-mini)               reading the message + writing the reply
   Twilio (via the CRM)              sending/receiving texts, delivery status
   CRM mail provider                 sending emails
   Vehicle Databases (API key)       OEM maintenance schedule per VIN, 1 credit
                                     per new VIN, stored on the vehicle
   NHTSA (public)                    safety recalls by year/make/model
   DealerVault (through the CRM)     deals, repair orders, service history

F. The AI's own records (same MongoDB, ai_* collections)
   ai_lead_state         stage, status (active / handoff / paused / opted out),
                         conversation state, after-hours choice, visit, bucket
   qualification_facts   what the customer told us (budget, timeline, trade...)
   ai_messages           every inbound/outbound message the AI handled
   scheduled_followups   every planned touch, reminder, resume, call timer
   ai_call_tasks         call tasks: assigned to, due, missed, outcome
   ai_consent            opt-outs / opt-ins per channel
   ai_compliance_log     every send decision with the rule that decided it
   ai_turn_log           every turn: what it read, decided, wrote, why
   ai_events             incoming events from the CRM
   ai_touches            follow-up touches and replies (insights)
   ai_price_snapshots    listed prices over time (verified price drops)
   ai_vehicle_ownership, ai_service_vehicles, ai_vehicle_recalls,
   ai_service_events     owned vehicles, maintenance, recalls, service history
   ai_customer_status    per-customer status across leads
   ai_send_checks        CRM campaign texts waiting for a compliance answer

G. What the AI writes back into the CRM
   POST /api/internal/ai/messages/send   send a text/email (CRM sends it)
   POST /api/internal/ai/messages        record a message in the conversation
   POST /api/internal/ai/leads/notes     notes on the lead (handoffs, service
                                         requests, after-handoff questions)
   POST /api/internal/ai/leads/status    CRM lead status (e.g. Contacted)
   POST /api/internal/ai/leads/dnd       do-not-contact after an opt-out
   POST /api/booking                     create / move an appointment
   The CRM reads back from the AI: /v1/call-tasks, /v1/staff/notices,
   /v1/insights, /v1/leads/... (AI Assistant pages).

4. HOW A LEAD ARRIVES, LEAD BUCKETS
================================================================================
  - Customer texts the dealership's number           -> lead created, AI answers
  - Lead email / ADF from a portal (CarGurus, Cars.com, KBB, ...) -> AI answers
  - Web form                                         -> AI answers
  - CSV import / DealerVault / historical record     -> OUTBOUND marketing:
    needs marketing consent; no consent = no text
  - Same customer twice (two forms, two portals)     -> only the OLDEST open
    lead runs the AI; the newer one is linked and paused; the customer's
    messages always land on the main one
  - Customer texts and no lead exists                -> a new lead is created;
    if an earlier lead was closed, it's a returning customer (name kept)

Lead buckets (by source; change the wording emphasis only, never the cadence):
  credit   - Capital One, Chase, Carzing, DealerCentric, Credit Application,
             eUnifi, 700Credit
  trade_in - KBB, TrueCar SELL My Car, AccuTrade, CarGurus SELL My Car
  general  - CarGurus, AutoTrader, Edmunds, Cars.com, Facebook, Instagram,
             AutoWeb, CarsDirect, TrueCar, and anything unknown
  If the customer's replies clearly show another intent, the bucket follows
  them (the original is kept for reporting). Service leads have no bucket.

5. THE MAIN FOLLOW-UP CADENCE (a lead nobody has converted yet)        [LIVE]
================================================================================
Every touch = TEXT + EMAIL together (+ a staff call task, section 10).
Touches go out at 10:00 dealer time, inside the legal texting window.

  Touch 1   immediately         first quality reply; opens "Hi X, this is
                                <AI name>, <Dealership>'s AI Sales Manager,
                                following up on your interest in the <Year Make
                                Model>. Tell me, what are you driving now?" (FTC,
                                Betsy's email 7 Oct) [FIX 9 OCT]
  Touch 2   3 hours later       name nudge: "{FirstName}?"
  Touch 3   Day 2               vehicle visual (photo if photos are on)
  Touch 4   Day 3               financing help
  Touch 5   Day 4               trade-in / appraisal
  Touch 6   Day 5               a verified feature or vehicle fact
  Touch 7   Day 6               why a visit is worth it
  Touch 8   Day 7               direct close
  Days 8-30                     one touch a week   (Day 14, 21, 28)  [BATCH 1]
                                price drops, similar new arrivals, "anything
                                holding you back?", why a visit is worth it
  Days 31-90                    every two weeks    (Day 42, 56, 70, 84) [BATCH 1]
                                "what did we miss?", credit tips, trade-in
                                market, still shopping? (time to get a yes/no)
  Day 91                        the opportunity closes (Closed - Lost)

  - A verified price drop on a car they looked at can replace a theme.
  - A customer reply doesn't use up a theme; the next unused one goes next.
  - Re-entry (e.g. after a no-show) starts a fresh schedule; the Day 91
    clock never resets.
  - The cadence stops as soon as something more important happens (s.6).
  - Max 3 marketing texts per customer per 24 h; held texts go at the next
    allowed time.

6. LEAD STAGES AND WHAT CONTROLS THE LEAD                              [LIVE]
================================================================================
  new_lead                        the lead arrived
  no_contact_made                 Touch 1 + the 3-hour nudge, no answer
  contact_made_no_next_action     customer replied, nothing scheduled -> cadence
  contact_made_specific_followup  customer gave a date ("call me Friday") ->
                                  AI follows up on that date, nothing before
  appointment_set                 a booking exists -> appointments (s.9)
  appointment_no_show             appointment + 1 hour, no visit
  sales_visit                     staff set Visited / Sold
  sold_pending / sold_delivered   manager outcomes (s.11, s.12)
  opted_out                       every channel stopped, or staff set DND
  closed_lost                     Day 91, or staff closed it
  closed_no_longer_owns           a delivered customer no longer owns the car

  Who is in charge of the conversation (separate from the stage):
  active (AI) / handoff (staff) / paused (staff) / opted_out.

  One workflow at a time. Priority when things collide:
  opt-out > sales visit > appointment > dated next step > no-show >
  contact without next step > no contact > new lead.
  The AI never closes a lead from a conversation; only Day 91 or staff do.

7. HOW THE AI TALKS (every reply)                                      [LIVE]
================================================================================
  - Answers what the customer asked FIRST, then at most ONE follow-up question.
  - Never re-asks something already known (a car they named settles
    "buy/trade/service?" and "new or used?").
  - Every question in a multi-question message gets an answer.
  - Steers towards an appointment: offers a visit to qualified or partly
    qualified leads, up to 3 attempts, each with a different angle.
  - Spanish in -> Spanish out.
  - Out-of-office / "driving" auto-replies get no reply.
  - Replies to campaign texts know which campaign they answer.
  - MULTI-AGENT [BATCH 2]: each reply is written by the specialist for what
    the customer is talking about right now:
      Sales             the car, features, stock, test drive (default)
      Price & payment   price, monthly payment, lease vs finance
      Credit            credit worries, approvals, down payment
      Trade-in          their trade, what it's worth, payoff
      Service           maintenance, repairs, recalls (service leads)
    Picked from the customer's words, then the lead type, then the bucket.
    Each has its own playbook and hard limits; all aim for the appointment;
    the same safety check applies to all.
  - Appointments: aims for the next 72 hours (the earliest open times are
    offered); a later date only when the customer asks for it  [BATCH 2]
  - Reads the lead's whole CRM conversation before every reply, including
    staff replies and internal staff notes (notes are never quoted to the
    customer)

  What the AI WILL say:
  - Real inventory (section 3D): year, miles, colour from the record
  - The LISTED PRICE of a car in stock, as a rough figure + range:
    "listed at about $38,400, roughly $37,500-$39,000; the team confirms the
    final number"
  - Dealer name, address, phone, opening hours (section 3A)
  - ONLY WHEN THE CUSTOMER ASKS, links from Dealer Setup:
      website                  "what's your website?"
      credit application link  "how do I apply?" / "send the finance app"
      trade-in appraisal link  "how do I value my trade online?"
    Never offered unasked (the goal is the visit). Missing in Dealer Setup ->
    "the team will send it". Any other link is blocked, except the page of a
    car the customer asked to see online.

  What the AI will NEVER say (always "the team will confirm"):
  - payments, financing terms, approvals, discounts, final price
  - trade-in value (no valuation provider connected)            [GAP]
  - anything not in the records (no invented specs, features,
    availability, urgency, "only one left")
  - "you're approved", "guaranteed", consent or eligibility claims
  - a birth year or the customer's age (birthday month/day only) [BATCH 2]
  - an open recall in a sales conversation (recalls are service-only)
  Every draft is checked in code before sending; a draft that breaks a rule is
  rewritten, and if it can't be made safe the lead goes to staff (soft handoff).

8. AFTER HOURS (dealership closed)                                     [LIVE]
================================================================================
Client decision 8 Oct 2026: the customer always gets an answer within seconds
and decides whether to continue now or wait for the dealership.

  - Customer texts at ANY hour (3 AM included) - new lead or a conversation
    already going: reply within ~20 seconds that answers them and asks:
      "...I can help you here now, or the team can pick this up when we open
       at 10:00 AM. Which would you like?"
  - Asked once per closed period (every night on its own; yesterday's answer
    doesn't count for tonight).

  Their answer:
    "now", a question, anything real   -> conversation continues at any hour
    "later"                            -> short thank-you, nothing until opening,
                                          then "Good morning... the team is in now"
    NO ANSWER                          -> default = wait: nothing until opening,
                                          then the opening message
    wants to visit                     -> no choice, straight to booking
    just "ok" / "thanks"               -> nothing
    dealership opens before they answer-> choice dropped, normal conversation
    texts again while waiting          -> answered and asked again

  Not covered by this (the business writes first):
  - Web-form / email / imported leads: the first message waits for the legal
    texting window (section 15).
  - Follow-up touches never go out at night (inside the window only).

9. APPOINTMENTS (stage: appointment_set)                               [LIVE]
================================================================================
All text + email, client's own wording:
  15 min after it's set / moved   confirmation: date, time, address, number
  each day before (not same-day)  "Counting down to our meeting at {dealership}!"
  the day before                  "...confirming our meeting for {date} at {time}.
                                   Does this time still work? Reply Y or N."
  appointment + 1 hour, no visit  No-show: "I am looking for you in the showroom -
                                   are you here and working with someone?"
  +24 h, no reply                 "How did everything go when you came in?"
  +24 h, no reply                 back to Contact Made - No Next Action -> cadence

  - The AI books sales appointments itself inside opening hours, against the
    capacity in AI Settings (bookings per slot, slot length), via the CRM's
    booking API - the same capacity staff and the booking page use.
  - Staff-made bookings get the same reminders.
  - Service visits are REQUESTED, not booked (section 13).

10. STAFF CALL TASKS AND WORK SCHEDULES                                [LIVE]
================================================================================
When a call task opens:
  - After each touch: a 60-minute timer; no meaningful reply -> a call task.
    A reply within 60 minutes cancels it.
  - Days 1-7 of a new lead: two call tasks per working day (morning: opening
    to noon; afternoon: noon to closing), max 2 a day. On/off: AI Settings.
  - Customer asked for a call: opens immediately, due within 5 minutes in
    opening hours; late -> alert to staff.

Who gets it:
  the lead's assigned salesperson, if working now
  salesperson off (their work schedule)  -> a colleague working now, the one
                                            with the fewest open tasks (the
                                            task says why)
  nobody assigned to the lead            -> the dealership's main account
  nobody working                         -> stays with the salesperson
  Leads are assigned to salespeople by hand in the CRM (Assign Leads).

Work schedules: Staff -> Add/Edit employee -> Work schedule.
  Default: "Same as the dealership's opening hours". Otherwise working days
  and shift times per day.

Calling rules (checked before a task shows): a valid phone; no voice opt-out;
no DND; 8:00-21:00 customer time AND dealer hours AND the state's own rules.
Outside them the task waits for the next allowed time.

Not done by the end of its window -> marked MISSED (shown per agent; BDC
report). No manager escalation yet [GAP].

11. SOLD PENDING (deal recorded, car not delivered)                    [LIVE]
================================================================================
  touches 1-4  weekly: Day 1, 8, 15, 22 after the outcome (10:00 dealer time)
  touch 5+     every 2 weeks: Day 36, 50, 64, ... while it stays pending
  No expiry. Stops only when staff set Sold - Delivered / Closed Lost, or
  the customer opts out. Each touch = text + email + 60-minute call task.
  Fixed client wording: never invents documents, delivery dates, financing
  status or reasons. Customer replies are answered under strict rules or
  routed to a person.

12. SOLD - DELIVERED (ownership / retention)                           [LIVE]
================================================================================
  Day 3          post-delivery check-in + first service offer
  yearly         birthday (only with a verified birth month/day)
  years 1-10     ownership anniversary: "Do you still have your [Model]?"
  on demand      maintenance reminders and safety recalls

  Maintenance: Vehicle Databases schedule by VIN (1 credit per new VIN);
    mileage only from the dealer's own records (repair orders, deals, staff);
    without a current mileage it uses time since the last visit and names no
    specific items. Never invents mileage or service items.
    WHICHEVER COMES FIRST, miles or months [BATCH 2]: a car driven little is
    still due when the months since its last visit run out (the schedule's
    own months when it has them, else 6), even before the miles.
    DealerVault service data (mileage, last visits) is read fresh on every
    daily recalculation; the OEM schedule itself is kept 180 days (it doesn't
    change, and each fetch costs a credit).
  Recalls: NHTSA checked regularly; a model-level match is a staff notice to
    verify first; only a VIN-confirmed open recall reaches the customer.
  "No longer own it" -> closed_no_longer_owns.
  Trade / equity / repurchase answers are collected, not acted on [NEXT SOW].

  12B. OWNER LIFE CYCLE FOR EVERY DEALERVAULT SALE                  [BATCH 2]
  Not only cars sold on a CRM lead: every delivered deal in DealerVault's
  sales file. Planned daily; text + email; every message says it is the AI.
    birthday         every year on the month/day (never the year)  Birth Date
    review+referral  Day 14 after delivery: a review, and "who's next in
                     line for a car?"                              Delivery Date
    first 30 days    Day 30: phone / Bluetooth / app set-up, offer
                     a visit with the salesperson                  Delivery Date
    first 90 days    Day 85 check-in                               Delivery Date
    anniversary      years 1-10 ("Can you believe it's been 3 years
                     with your RAV4?")                             Delivery Date
    lease end        90 days before the lease ends                 Deal Type,
                                                                   Term, First
                                                                   Pay Date
    paid off         when the finance contract ends
                     ("congratulations, you paid off your car")    Term, Amount
                                                                   Financed
    tips             every 60 days in year 1, then every 90, for
                     5 years (service, ownership, safety tips)     Delivery Date
  Conditions:
    - goes out on the customer's latest CRM lead; a DealerVault customer with
      NO lead in the CRM is counted and skipped                       [GAP]
    - waits a week while that customer is in an active sales conversation
      (birthday excepted); waits a day while staff are talking to them
    - stops when they opt out or say they no longer own the car; unwound /
      cancelled deals are skipped
    - a touch more than 3 weeks late is skipped, never sent late
    - a car sold on a CRM lead keeps its anniversary in section 12 (no double)
  Day counts above are AutoPulse defaults until the client changes them.

13. SERVICE                                                            [LIVE]
================================================================================
  - The AI offers a service visit and asks which day/time suits them.
  - Their answer becomes a SERVICE REQUEST with notes for the service team
    (day/time, vehicle, mileage, needs, loaner, waiting): a staff notice
    (AI Alerts) and a note on the lead. Nothing is booked.
  - "What's my next service?" is answered only from the stored schedule and
    real mileage; otherwise the team confirms.
  - Reading/writing the real service scheduler (Dealer FX)  [NEXT SOW]
  - SERVICE NOT SOLD / DECLINED SERVICES [BATCH 2]: a repair order that
    closed with technician recommendations gets ONE follow-up 14 days later,
    quoting the recommendations word for word ("our technicians recommended:
    Replace front brake pads; Rotate tires. Would you like us to take care of
    that?"). Not sent if the car has been back for service since. A "yes" is
    a service request for the service team (nothing booked).

14. HANDOFFS - WHEN THE AI STOPS AND A PERSON TAKES OVER               [LIVE]
================================================================================
  Customer asks for a person:
    said how ("call me")      -> call task now (section 10), handoff
    didn't say how            -> one reply offering a call or a text from a
                                 team member (nothing else in that reply)
    answer "call"             -> call task, handoff
    answer "text"             -> staff notice, handoff
    "call me at 9:30"         -> call task; the AI KEEPS ANSWERING their
                                 questions meanwhile; staff note "the call is
                                 still yours"
  Hard handoff (customer clearly upset, urgent, needs a manager's decision):
    the AI goes quiet. After 30 business minutes: one "the team has your
    message" text + staff alert. Later messages: a holding reply at most every
    2 hours (a question or booking request gets one within 10 minutes) and a
    staff notice + note with what they asked.
  Soft handoff (the AI couldn't write a safe reply, or read the customer as
    upset): quiet, but takes the lead back if the customer asks to book.
  Staff pause the AI on a lead: quiet until staff resume.
  Customer asks for a person but staff never act -> see [GAP]s, section 17.
  MANAGER ALERTS [BATCH 1]: the manager in Dealer Setup (general manager
    email + phone, else the store phone) gets an EMAIL and a TEXT for:
    every escalation (Managerial Review, by staff or the AI), a requested
    call nobody made, a customer writing while staff own the lead, and a
    customer no longer interested. The dashboard card is "AI Alerts".

15. COMPLIANCE (TCPA) AND OPT-OUT                                      [LIVE]
================================================================================
When the business writes first (follow-ups, the first message to a web-form /
email / imported lead, reminders):
  - State texting windows in the customer's time zone (from ZIP, else
    DealerVault state, else area code; unknown = the strictest of all).
    NY, FL: from 8:00 AM. Some states: no Sundays, no holidays (incl. state
    holidays), stricter weekday hours. Held messages go at the next allowed time.
  - Max 3 marketing texts per customer per 24 h (state caps may be tighter).
  - Indiana / Maine automated-device rules applied to every AI text.
  - New Jersey: no unsolicited sales texts to cell phones (only follow-ups to
    their own inquiry, or with consent).
  - Imported / DealerVault / CSV leads need marketing consent.
  - Campaign texts (sent by the CRM) pass the same check first.
Replies to the customer's own message (including the first reply when the
customer texted first): allowed at any hour.
AI voice calls: off. Only people call.
Every decision is logged with its rule (ai_compliance_log).

Opt-out and back in:
  STOP / UNSUBSCRIBE (alone)         -> that channel stops (carrier confirms)
  "don't text me", "leave me alone"  -> that channel / every channel; one
                                        plain confirmation
  "not interested", "not right now"  -> NOT an opt-out (an objection)
  "not interested", no reason        -> asks why once, then stops
  START / UNSTOP / "YES" (capitals)  -> back in on that channel
  "you can text me again"            -> back in
  Wrong number                       -> stops and flags the number
  A possible opt-out (unclear wording) -> nothing marketing goes out until it's
                                        resolved by the customer or staff
